# Raekolon  
Pronounced (R-eɪ-k-oʊ-lon). This section of land on the planet is old, very old. The continent is named for Raekos (Ray-Ko-s), who at one point conquered the entirety of these lands. Scholars debate on the naming of the empire's rule but most use the Pelorian Empire for the civilization primarily worshipped Pelor, The Dawn Father.
