1 Can of garbanzo
1 Lemon
1 Hand full of Ice
1 Garlic Clove
1 Tahini Sauce
1 tsp cumin
Olive Oil
Salt

Drain chickpeas and boil in new water
transfer to bigger pan and add cold water, skim shells off top
put chickpeas in food processor and add garlic clove and blend.
Once slightly blended and some tahini and cumin blend again.
Add lemon and ice cubes and blend again, once smooth add salt and olive oil again.
